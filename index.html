Our History

AL-SHABEER INTERNATIONAL: Legacy of Excellence

Our story started in 1957. Our founder, Mr. Shabbir Bhutta, started the natural sheep casing business with great determination. By working hard to overcome early problems, he built a trusted name in the local market. His focus was always on top quality, new ideas, and keeping customers happy.

1957 – 1980

The Founding

In the beginning, Mr. Shabbir Bhutta worked day and night to provide the best products. His hard work gave the company a strong start. This helped the business grow steadily and earn a great reputation.

1980 – 1997

Expansion & Growth

As the business did well, we made our operations bigger. We used modern methods to work faster and make more products. This helped us meet the high demand for sheep casings and become a top company in the market.

1997 – 2018

New Era of Leadership

In 1997, Mr. Pervaiz Bhutta became the leader. Under his guidance, the company grew faster than ever before. With fresh marketing ideas and strong partnerships, we added new products and entered new markets.

2018 – Present

International Expansion

In 2018, the third generation of our family joined the business with new ideas. We officially formed "AL-SHABEER INTERNATIONAL" to take our business worldwide. Today, we proudly export our high-quality natural sheep casings to customers in many different countries.

Legacy Of Quality And Excellence

From the start, we have always focused on doing our best. This hard work has earned us respect and the trust of our partners. We continue to follow the path set by Mr. Shabbir Bhutta, working hard to make a lasting mark in the global market.
